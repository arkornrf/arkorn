Motivation

FROM COMPLEXITY TO CLARITY

Systems thinking came before the tools.

My work began with complex human systems: movement, rehabilitation, nutrition, and long-term behavior change.

Ten years of applied practice taught me to look past symptoms, map relationships, explain difficult ideas clearly, and iterate from real feedback — not assumptions.

AI automation is a natural extension of that approach. I study how a process actually works, where attention is wasted, and which decisions should stay human. Then I design a smaller, testable system around it.

A RESEARCH-LED PROCESS

Understand first. Automate second.

The goal is not to add AI everywhere. It is to remove avoidable friction while keeping the workflow understandable and easy to correct.

01
